# Copy radio serial number or IP address to clipboard for support

This page helps you quickly copy your FLEX-8600’s serial number, IP address, or other identifying information to your system clipboard so you can paste it into a support ticket or email.

## Before you start

- Your radio must be connected to AetherSDR.

## Steps

### Copy the radio serial number

1. Open `Settings > Radio Setup...`.
2. On the **Radio** tab, locate the **Radio SN** read-only label.
3. Click the clipboard copy button (tray icon) immediately to the right of the serial number value.

### Copy the radio IP address

1. Open `Settings > Radio Setup...`.
2. Click the **Network** tab.
3. Locate the **IP Address** read-only label.
4. Click the clipboard copy button (tray icon) immediately to the right of the IP address value.

### Copy the MAC address

1. Open `Settings > Radio Setup...`.
2. Click the **Network** tab.
3. Locate the **MAC Address** read-only label.
4. Click the clipboard copy button (tray icon) immediately to the right of the MAC address.

### Copy the firmware version or license info

1. Open `Settings > Radio Setup...`.
2. On the **Radio** tab, scroll to the **License Info** section.
3. Click the clipboard copy button next to any field: **Subscription**, **Expiration**, **Radio ID**, or **Licensed version**.

You can also copy **HW Version**, **Model**, or **Options** from the **Radio** tab the same way.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Clipboard copy button (tray icon) | A small icon next to each read-only value. Clicking it copies that value to the system clipboard and briefly shows a “Copied” popup. The button appears dimmed when the value is empty or unavailable. |

## Tips

- Each copy button is labeled with the field name in its tooltip (e.g., “Copy Radio SN”). Hover to confirm which value will be copied before clicking.
- The “Copied” popup appears for one second near the button.
