# Tune to a net with one click

Use the Net Scheduler to instantly tune your active slice to a scheduled net's frequency, mode, and filter settings.

## Before you start

- The Net Scheduler must contain at least one net entry (see [Create a recurring net schedule](create-a-recurring-net-schedule.md)).
- A radio connection is required to transmit tuning commands.

## Steps

1. Open the Net Scheduler: `Tools > Net Scheduler...`.
2. Select the net you want to tune to in the Net table.
3. Click **Tune Now**.

The active slice immediately changes to the net's stored frequency, mode, and filter.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| **Tune Now** | Tunes the active slice to the selected net's frequency with the stored mode and filter settings. Uses the same memory recall policy as memory recalls. |
| **Net table** | Double-click any net entry to perform the same one-click tuning action as **Tune Now**. |
