# Capture the current frequency as a net preset

Quickly save your current VFO frequency, mode, and filter settings as a tuning preset for a net entry in the Net Scheduler. This saves you from manually typing in frequency and mode details.

## Before you start

- Your radio must be connected.
- The Net Scheduler dialog must be open (`Tools > Net Scheduler...`).

## Steps

1. Tune your radio to the desired frequency and set the mode and filter as you want them saved for the net.
2. In the Net Scheduler dialog, click **Capture Current**.
3. The current VFO frequency, mode, and filter are immediately stored as the tuning preset for the selected net entry. If no net is selected, a new net entry is created with those values.

## What each control does

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Capture Current** | Button. Captures the active slice's frequency, mode, and filter settings and assigns them to the currently selected net entry (or creates a new entry if none is selected). |

## Tips

- You can use **Capture Current** on an existing net entry to update its tuning preset without changing the net's name, recurrence rule, or reminder.
- This is especially useful when you see net info posted on a DX cluster or during a contest and want to quickly save a spot to your schedule.
