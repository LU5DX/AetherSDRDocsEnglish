# Adjust master speaker volume

The Title Bar provides a **Master volume** slider that controls the line-out audio level for your radio's speaker output. Use it to raise or lower speaker volume without leaving the main window.

## Steps

1. Locate the **🔊 (line out mute)** button in the Title Bar. If the icon shows 🔇, click it to unmute before adjusting volume.
2. Drag the **Master volume** slider left to decrease or right to increase the line-out audio level. The numeric label to the right of the slider updates immediately to show the current value (0–100).

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **🔊 (line out mute)** | Click to mute or unmute line-out speaker audio. Icon changes to 🔇 when muted. |
| **Master volume** | Drag to set the line-out audio level from 0 to 100. Default is 100. The current value appears in a numeric label to the right of the slider. Setting is saved as `MasterVolume` and persists across sessions. |

## Tips

- The **Master volume** value is saved automatically — the slider position is restored the next time you launch AetherSDR.
- To silence audio instantly without changing the slider position, use the **🔊 (line out mute)** button instead of dragging the slider to 0.
- To adjust headphone audio independently, use the **Headphone volume** slider and **🎧 (headphone mute)** button to the right of the master controls.
