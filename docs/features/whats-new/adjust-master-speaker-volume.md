# Adjust master speaker volume

The Title Bar provides a master volume slider and mute toggle that control the line-out speaker audio level. Changes take effect immediately and the volume setting persists across sessions.

## Steps

1. Locate the **🔊 (line out mute)** toggle button in the Title Bar. If the icon shows 🔇, click it once to unmute before adjusting volume.
2. Drag the **Master volume** slider left or right to set the desired level (0–100). The numeric label to the right of the slider updates as you drag. The default value is **100**.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **🔊 (line out mute)** | Click to mute or unmute line-out speaker audio. Icon changes to 🔇 when muted. |
| **Master volume** | Sets the line-out audio level (0–100). Current value is shown in a numeric label to the right. Defaults to 100. Persisted via `MasterVolume`. |

## Tips

- Muting with **🔊 (line out mute)** does not change the saved **Master volume** value — when you unmute, audio resumes at the level shown on the slider.
- To silence audio without losing your volume setting, prefer the mute toggle over dragging the slider to zero.
