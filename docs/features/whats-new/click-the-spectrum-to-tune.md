# Click the spectrum to tune

The Spectrum / Waterfall widget lets you retune your receiver by clicking directly on the spectrum line or waterfall. It is always visible inside each Panadapter applet.

## Steps

1. Locate the Spectrum / Waterfall display inside any open Panadapter applet.
2. Click on the frequency position you want to tune to. The receiver tunes to that point immediately.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Waterfall colour scheme | Changes the gradient palette used to map signal intensity to waterfall colour. Choose from Default (0), Grayscale (1), Blue-Green (2), Fire (3), or Plasma (4). Setting is saved per panadapter. |

## Tips

- Click anywhere on the waterfall (scrolling history) as well as the live spectrum line — both respond to tune clicks.
- To fine-tune, drag the filter edges left or right after clicking to set the centre frequency.
- Scroll the mouse wheel over the spectrum to zoom in or out, which makes it easier to click precisely on a narrow signal.
