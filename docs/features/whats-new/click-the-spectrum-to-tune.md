# Click the spectrum to tune

The Spectrum / Waterfall widget renders a live FFT spectrum line and scrolling waterfall for each panadapter. Click anywhere on the spectrum or waterfall to tune the radio to that frequency.

## Steps

1. Locate the Spectrum / Waterfall display inside any open Panadapter applet.
2. Click the frequency position on the spectrum line or waterfall you want to tune to. The receiver moves to that frequency immediately.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Waterfall colour scheme | Changes the gradient palette used to map signal intensity to waterfall colour. Choose from Default (0), Grayscale (1), Blue-Green (2), Fire (3), or Plasma (4). The selection is saved per panadapter. |

## Tips

- You can also drag the filter edges on the spectrum to reshape the passband without retuning.
- Scroll the mouse wheel over the spectrum to zoom the frequency span in or out.
- The waterfall colour scheme default is **Default (0)**; change it if signals are hard to distinguish against the background.
