# Toggle PC audio input on or off

The **PC Audio** button in the Title Bar enables or disables your PC microphone audio input. The setting persists across sessions so your preference is remembered when you reopen the application.

## Steps

1. Look at the **PC Audio** toggle button in the Title Bar at the top of the main window. When lit, PC audio input is enabled (default: on).
2. Click **PC Audio** to toggle it off. Click it again to turn it back on.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **PC Audio** | Enables or disables PC microphone audio input. Default is on. Persists across sessions via `PcAudioEnabled`. |

## Tips

- If you are not transmitting or do not want the PC microphone active during a session, turn **PC Audio** off to prevent accidental audio input.
- The state is saved automatically — you do not need to confirm or save manually.
