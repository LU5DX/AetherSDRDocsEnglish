# Toggle PC audio input on or off

The Title Bar provides a **PC Audio** toggle button that enables or disables your PC microphone audio input. The setting persists across sessions so your preference is remembered the next time you launch AetherSDR.

## Steps

1. Locate the **PC Audio** button in the Title Bar at the top of the main window.
2. Click **PC Audio** to toggle PC microphone audio input on or off. The button is enabled by default; click once to disable, click again to re-enable.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| **PC Audio** | Enables or disables PC microphone audio input. Default: on. Persists across sessions via `PcAudioEnabled`. |

## Tips

- If you are not transmitting and want to prevent accidental microphone input, turn **PC Audio** off. Turn it back on before your next transmission.
