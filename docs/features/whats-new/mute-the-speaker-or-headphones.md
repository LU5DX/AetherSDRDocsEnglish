# Mute the speaker or headphones

The Title Bar provides separate mute toggles for the line-out speaker and headphones, letting you silence either output without changing the volume level.

## Steps

1. To mute the speaker, click the **🔊 (line out mute)** button in the Title Bar. The icon changes to 🔇 when muted. Click again to unmute.
2. To mute the headphones, click the **🎧 (headphone mute)** button in the Title Bar. The icon changes to 🔇 when muted. Click again to unmute.

## What each control does

| Control | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **🔊 (line out mute)** | Toggles mute on the line-out speaker audio. Icon changes to 🔇 when muted. |
| **Master volume** | Sets the line-out audio level from 0 to 100 (default: 100). The current value appears in a numeric label to the right of the slider. |
| **🎧 (headphone mute)** | Toggles mute on the headphone audio. Icon changes to 🔇 when muted. |
| **Headphone volume** | Sets the headphone audio level from 0 to 100 (default: 50). The current value appears in a numeric label to the right of the slider. |

## Tips

- Muting does not change the slider position — your previous volume level is restored when you unmute.
- To reduce volume gradually instead of silencing entirely, drag the **Master volume** or **Headphone volume** slider to the left.
