# Drag a filter edge to reshape the passband

The Spectrum / Waterfall widget lets you drag the edges of the filter passband overlay directly on the spectrum display to change the filter bandwidth and shape for the active receiver.

## Steps

1. Open the Panadapter applet. The spectrum display is always visible inside it.
2. Move your mouse pointer over the left or right edge of the shaded filter passband overlay on the spectrum. The cursor changes to a horizontal resize cursor when you are over a draggable edge.
3. Click and drag the edge left or right to widen or narrow the passband. Release the mouse button to confirm the new filter boundary.

## Tips

- Drag the left edge to adjust the low-frequency cutoff; drag the right edge to adjust the high-frequency cutoff.
- For fine adjustments, zoom into the spectrum first by scrolling the mouse wheel over the display to expand the frequency scale.
