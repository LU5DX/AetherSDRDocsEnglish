# Enable peak-hold for SSB voice peaks

Peak-hold freezes the needle at its highest deflection so you can read SSB voice peaks without having to watch the meter continuously.

## Before you start

- You must be connected to a FLEX-8600 radio.
- The Cross-Needle Meter applet must be open (Applet panel > Cross-Needle tile).

## Steps

1. Locate the **Peak hold** toggle button near the meter face.
2. Click **Peak hold** to turn it On.

The needle now holds at its peak position, then decays when the incoming power drops.

## What each control does

| Control | Type | Default | Setting key |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Peak hold | Toggle button | Off | (none persisted) |

## Tips

- Peak-hold is most useful in SSB; for AM or FM with constant carrier you may prefer it Off for instant response.
